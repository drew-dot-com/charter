Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy covers the publishing tool that TOON ("we") uses to post to its own official accounts: the TOON Protocol Page on LinkedIn and @toonprotocol on X. The tool is used only by us, for our own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What the tool does

  • Publishes posts written for the official TOON accounts.
  • Reads statistics about those posts, such as impressions, clicks, reactions, comments and shares, so we can see which posts are useful to readers and write better ones.

What we collect

  • The posts we publish and their identifiers.
  • Statistics for our own posts and Page, as totals. We do not request or store lists of the individual members who viewed, reacted to or followed our accounts.
  • The access tokens LinkedIn and X issue to us so the tool can act for our accounts.

We do not collect personal data about LinkedIn or X members, build profiles of anyone, use the data for advertising, or sell or share it with anyone.

Where it is kept

Post records and statistics are stored on a server we control. Access tokens are kept as secrets on that server, never in source code or public files. Statistics are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Tokens are deleted when they expire or when access is revoked.

Revoking access

Any administrator of a TOON account can revoke the tool's access at any time from that platform's settings for connected apps. The tool stops working for that account immediately.

Changes

If the tool starts collecting anything beyond what is listed here, we will update this page first and change the date at the top.
